# Import / Export (Trade)

**Headline:** Track every shipment, document, cost and payment — and see the true landed cost.
**Tagline:** For importers, exporters, traders and freight businesses. Works on its own.

## Features
- **Shipments** — import or export; statuses booked → in transit → arrived → customs → cleared → delivered → closed; goods lines (HS code, quantity, weight, volume), containers, milestone history.
- **Parties** — suppliers, buyers, forwarders, clearing agents, carriers, banks.
- **Documents** — attach files (up to 8 MB), expiry watched (L/C, permits, certificates).
- **Costs and landed cost** — freight, insurance, duty, etc. shared across goods lines by value, weight, volume or quantity, converted to your currency.
- **Payments** — advance, balance, L/C, TT with due dates.
- **Duty rates** — per-HS-code rates (longest matching code wins) and a **duty estimate** with the working shown; unmatched lines are flagged, never treated as duty-free.
- **Stock in transit** — incoming/outgoing quantity per product; badge on the Inventory stock drawer.
- **Dashboard & daily alerts** — late ETAs, port free days used up (demurrage), expiring documents, payments due/overdue (in-app notifications).
- **Reports** — 8 reports in the Reports hub.
- Commercial invoice and packing list PDFs; **goods import** from Excel/CSV; optional link to a Purchase or Sales order to copy its lines.
- Costs and payments are separate permissions, so staff can track cargo without seeing what it cost.
- Available automatically for tenants whose industry is Import & Export.
- AI assistant tools (15) for shipments, parties, costs, payments.

## Do not claim yet
- Posting landed cost or payments to Finance; updating Inventory cost from landed cost; email/push alerts; bulk import of whole shipments; per-cost currency conversion.
