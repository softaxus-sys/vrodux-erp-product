# Hospitality — Hotel PMS

**Headline:** Run the front desk, rooms, rates and housekeeping from one screen.
**Tagline:** Reservations, room calendar, folios, seasonal rates and housekeeping for hotels and serviced apartments.

## Features
- **Front Desk** — arrivals, in-house, departures; check-in (optionally change room), check-out (blocks with an unpaid balance unless allowed), no-show, cancel with reason and fee, amend dates (re-prices), move room.
- **Room Calendar (tape chart)** and **Reservations** list.
- **Rooms** — rooms, **room types**, out-of-order blocks. Availability respects blocks and cancelled stays.
- **Rates** — rate plans (meal plan, refundable, a default "BAR"), seasonal weekday/weekend rates per room type, minimum stay. Bookings keep the rates they were sold at (a price per night is stored).
- **Guests** — guest profiles kept across stays; returning guests are found by id, email or phone.
- **Folio** — room charges, extras, voidable charges, payments, discount; GCC-style totals with service charge %, tax % and a fixed nightly fee; printed bill.
- **Housekeeping** — tasks, created automatically on check-out.
- **Hotel settings** — check-in/out times, weekend days, service %, tax %.
- Hotel payments can be posted to Finance (Finance Posting).

## Access control
Permissions for bookings, rooms, rates, guests, folio, housekeeping, settings.

## Do not claim yet
- Channel manager / OTA sync, online booking engine, door-lock or payment-terminal integrations.
- The tape chart shades Fri/Sat as weekend regardless of your setting.
- Not yet browser-verified end to end with a signed-in session.
