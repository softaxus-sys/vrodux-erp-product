# Sales

**Headline:** Quotes that look professional, orders that flow into delivery and invoicing.
**Tagline:** From quotation to delivery challan — with a customer link they can accept online.

## Features
**Quotations**
- Full document: title, reference, cover note, sections, line items with units and notes, optional extras (shown but not charged), header discount, tax, payment terms, terms & conditions, prepared-by, validity.
- Custom fields (e.g. project code, lead time, warranty) without any setup.
- Templates: save your boilerplate and prefilled lines; a default "Standard Quotation" is created for each workspace. Editing a template never changes quotations already sent.
- Letterhead pulled from your company settings (logo, name, address, TRN, signature, stamp, accent colour).
- Send by email (with the link) or share a link; customer can **accept or decline online**; you see sent / opened / answered with dates; accepted/declined alerts reach the creator and the lead owner.
- Print/PDF, duplicate as a new revision, convert to a sales order, create/link a Finance invoice (needs Finance).
- Create from a CRM lead or account (needs CRM); quotes follow a converted lead onto its new account.

**Sales orders** — confirm, track status, delete drafts.
**Delivery challans** — record deliveries against an order; the order moves to shipped / delivered as quantities are delivered.
**Sales returns** — approve or reject returns.
**Customers** — customer master for sales documents.

## Works with
CRM, Finance (invoices; sales returns can be posted via the Finance posting queue), Inventory, Manufacturing ("Plan production" from a sales order), Import/Export, notifications.

## Access control
Permissions for quotations, orders, returns (view/create/edit/delete/approve).

## Do not claim yet
- Server-generated PDF attached to the email (the customer link has Download PDF).
- Quotation "send to customer" and "refund" buttons beyond what is listed.
- Deleting a quotation revokes its customer link (this is intentional behaviour, not a bug).
