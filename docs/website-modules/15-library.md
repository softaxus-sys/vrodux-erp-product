# Library Management

**Headline:** Catalogue, lending, fines and a member portal — across all your branches.
**Tagline:** For school, university, corporate and community libraries. Works on its own.

## Features
- **Catalogue** — titles (ISBN, authors, category, type, replacement cost) and individual copies with barcode, branch, shelf, condition; **bulk import** from Excel/CSV (safe to re-run).
- **Circulation desk** — issue/return with a barcode scanner, renew, mark lost, reservations (queue per title; copy held for the next member for 3 days), **transfers** between branches (in transit → received).
- **Members** — membership types with borrowing rules (items, days, renewals, fine per day, reservations), expiry, suspend; same-title reserve wait configurable.
- **Fines** — automatic overdue and lost-item fines, payments, waivers; optional **post to Finance** (draft journal entry).
- **Reminders (email)** — due soon, overdue, ready for pickup; one email per member per kind; off until switched on.
- **Branches & staff** — staff can be limited to their own branch(es); managers never limited.
- **Member portal** — public page per library: card number + PIN sign-in, see loans, renew, search, reserve, cancel, see fines. Off until the library opens it.
- **Printing** — barcode labels (A4 sheets or label-printer rolls, Code 128) and member cards.
- **Reports** — 12 library reports in the Reports hub; dashboard.

## Do not claim yet
- SMS/WhatsApp reminders, Arabic reminder emails and an **Arabic member portal (English only)**.
- Member self-registration, PIN reset by email, paying fines online, spine labels, QR codes, e-books.
- Barcode printing not yet tested on real label stock.
