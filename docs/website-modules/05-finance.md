# Finance & Accounting

**Headline:** Books you can trust, with every module feeding the ledger — under your control.
**Tagline:** Invoicing, expenses, banking, general ledger, UAE VAT, and an Integration Center that posts other modules' transactions.

## Features
**Invoicing**
- Invoices (send, mark paid, cancel), customer and supplier records, receipt and payment vouchers, receivables/payables.
- Tax invoice layout: seller's full name, registered address and TRN; buyer's address and TRN; "TAX INVOICE" title when a TRN is set — on print, PDF and email.
- **Recurring invoices**: monthly/other schedules, CC addresses, **auto-email** to the customer with a PDF, per-template auto-send switch.
- **Recurring expenses**: generate expenses on a schedule; either review each one, or auto-post (approve, pay and post to the ledger) per template; debit/credit accounts selectable.

**Expenses & purchase bills** — approve/reject/pay; purchase bills for AP including import bills in foreign currency.

**Banking** — bank accounts, transactions, **reconciliation** (link a bank account to a ledger account, match statement lines to journal lines, one-click auto-match when unambiguous, unmatch).

**Accounting** — chart of accounts, journals (post/void), fiscal periods, exchange rates (USD base, live daily refresh), general ledger, trial balance, P&L, balance sheet, cash flow, budgets.

**Currency** — your operating currency is set per company; ledger postings convert into it.

**Tax**
- VAT screens and a **UAE VAT 201 return preparation** workspace: boxes computed from your ledger, validation, reconciliation to the VAT control account, FAF CSV and XML export, workflow draft → approved → ready, and "filed via portal" with your EmaraTax reference.

**Integration Center / Finance Posting**
- See how each module reaches the ledger. Seven sources queue documents (POS sales/refunds, restaurant orders/refunds, real-estate rent, library fines, payroll) plus sales returns, wallet top-ups, POS cash in/out and hotel payments.
- Modes per source: off, **review** (queue and post manually) or **auto** (with start date and explicit acknowledgement).
- Safeguards: a check before posting (shows problems per document), control totals, held-for-review for unreadable data, duplicate-posting guard, reversal with reason, approval limits and optional second approver, only licensed modules appear.
- Customisable account mapping per role.

## Works with
Everything: POS, Restaurant, Real Estate, Library, HR payroll, Hospitality, Sales, Visa (invoices), Manufacturing (cost posting), Library fines.

## Access control
Fine-grained keys for invoicing, expenses, accounting, journals, GL, budgeting, banking, tax, payroll approval, integrations (view / submit / approve / approve-high).

## Do not claim yet
- **No direct submission to the FTA.** The FTA publishes no VAT-return API; Vrodux prepares and exports files. XML is not FTA-certified.
- Bank statement file import and one-to-many matching.
- Inventory valuation / goods receipts / stock adjustments / production cost posting to the ledger automatically.
- E-invoicing submission (groundwork only).
- Tips, payroll withholdings default to a liability account — an accountant should confirm mappings before switching any source to automatic.
