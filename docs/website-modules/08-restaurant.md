# Restaurant

**Headline:** From table to kitchen to payment — floor, kitchen display, delivery and loyalty.
**Tagline:** Dine-in, takeaway and delivery with split bills, tips, modifiers and rider tracking.

## Features
- **Floor & tables** — floors, dining areas, tables, floor designer.
- **Order screen** — dine-in/takeaway/delivery, categories, structured **modifiers** (priced, required/optional, min/max), special instructions, hold/recall.
- **Kitchen Display** — tickets with mark ready / served; kitchen configuration.
- **Bill & pay** — split payment methods, **split bills by item**, **tips**, discounts with reason, refunds, voids with reason (every discount/void/refund is audited), tax receipt.
- **Menu management** — categories, dishes, photos, availability toggle, **bulk import from Excel/CSV** (with photos by URL); a 62-dish sample menu is included for demos.
- **Delivery** — dispatcher board, riders linked to logins, a mobile-friendly **rider screen** (navigate, call, amount to collect), delivery orders punched on the order screen.
- **Customers & loyalty** — link a customer to a bill, earn points (1 point per 100 spent), redeem as a discount; wallet and house account payments.
- **Shifts** — orders attach to the cashier's open shift so payments land in the Z-report; waiters don't need their own till.
- **Roles** — Restaurant Manager, Staff, Cashier, Waiter, Kitchen Staff, Delivery Rider.
- **Reports and dashboards**; QR table ordering and tracking links.

## Works with
POS shifts, Finance posting queue (orders, refunds), CRM customers, Recipe costing.

## Access control
Permissions for tables, menu, orders (void / discount / refund), kitchen, reservations, delivery, reports.

## Do not claim yet
- Rider on/off-duty, configurable loyalty rates, automatic customer/group discounts.
- A rider marking "delivered" does not change the kitchen order status.
- Partial-quantity bill splitting (whole lines only); a Modifier Groups admin screen (API only).
