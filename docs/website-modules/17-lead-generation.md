# Lead Generation

**Headline:** Find businesses, reveal their contacts, and email them — from your own mailbox.
**Tagline:** Prospecting and cold-email outreach with a reply inbox. UAE live.

## Features
- **Find** — search real businesses by category and area (Google Places + website enrichment); discovery is free.
- **Reveal with credits** — pay credits only to reveal a contact; credits are refunded for unusable or bounced contacts. Buy credit packs by Stripe or PayPal.
- **Prospects** — list, edit, import/export, push to your CRM.
- **Campaigns** — multi-step cold-email sequences sent from **your own connected mailbox** (Google, Microsoft or SMTP/IMAP), with warm-up limits, send window in your time zone, unsubscribe link and sender address on every email, suppression lists, auto-pause on bounces/complaints. Line breaks in templates are reflowed so sentences don't break mid-line.
- **Inbox** — replies in one place, classified.
- **Settings** — business profile (prefilled from your company settings and industry: offer, ideal customers, preferred business types).
- **Super-admin console** — packs, platform settings, credit grants, usage.
- Dedicated permissions (reveal, send, etc.) per role.

## Do not claim yet
- Countries other than UAE (catalogued, not live).
- Gmail connection for the public needs Google's app verification; Microsoft connection and an email verifier require configuration.
- Own-domain DNS sending, resold mailboxes, calls, SMS/WhatsApp, Arabic emails.
- Not yet end-to-end verified in production; Stripe webhook payload parsing for the newest API version is unconfirmed.
- Cold email must comply with local law and recipients' consent expectations; say so.
