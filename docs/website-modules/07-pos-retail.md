# Retail POS

**Headline:** A till that keeps selling when the internet doesn't.
**Tagline:** Shifts, sales, refunds, credit accounts, a live dashboard and offline mode.

## Features
- **Cashier and retail POS screens** with barcode scanning, hold/recall, shift open/close with cash counting and Z-report, cash in/out with categories.
- **Refunds, voids, discounts** controlled by separate permissions (a cashier role can't refund unless granted). Voiding returns stock correctly for both inventory- and POS-sourced products.
- **Customers** — customer book, wallet/store credit, loyalty, vouchers.
- **Buy on credit (house account)** — per-customer credit limit and terms, due dates, ageing buckets (not due, 1–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+), collect payments, statement PDF, stop-credit, WhatsApp reminder link, daily **email reminders** (off until you switch on), Credit Control page.
- **POS dashboard** — gross/net sales, refunds, voids, trend vs previous period, payment mix, top products and cashiers, open shifts.
- **Offline mode (per workspace switch)** — tills record shifts, sales, refunds, voids and cash movements locally and upload with **Sync to Cloud** at day end. Safe to sync twice. Switching modes requires everything synced. Offline sales are never rejected for stock; negative stock is flagged. Browser and desktop app.
- **Master data** — currencies, tax rates, payment terms, customer groups, vendors.
- **Reports** — 19 POS reports in the Reports hub.

## Works with
Inventory (stock deduction), Finance posting queue (sales, refunds, wallet, cash movements), Restaurant (shared shifts), CRM customers.

## Access control
Cashier / Supervisor / Manager tiers; permissions on sales, refund, void, discount, sessions (open/approve), products, customers, reports.

## Do not claim yet
- Vouchers, loyalty and customer search are not available offline; product add needs a connection.
- Voiding a pre-offline server sale while offline.
- Receipts in Arabic; SMS sending of credit reminders.
