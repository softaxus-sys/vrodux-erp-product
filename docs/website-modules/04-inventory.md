# Inventory

**Headline:** Know what you have, where it is, and when to reorder.
**Tagline:** Products, warehouses, stock movements and transfers.

## Features
- **Products** — paginated, filterable, barcode lookup, activate/deactivate, SKU unique per business.
- **Master data** — brands, product categories, units of measure.
- **Warehouses** — multiple warehouses, set a default.
- **Stock movements** — receipts, write-offs, adjustments, count corrections (plus production issue/receipt from Manufacturing).
- **Stock transfers** — create → submit → approve → receive between warehouses.
- **Reports** — 17 inventory reports in the Reports hub; low-stock view.
- **Stock in transit** (with Import/Export) — an "On its way" box on a product shows incoming cargo.

## Works with
Purchase (receipts), Sales (deliveries), POS, Manufacturing (BOM component issue and finished goods receipt), Import/Export.

## Access control
Permissions for stock, warehouses, movements, transfers.

## Do not claim yet
- Inventory valuation posted into the general ledger (inventory is not carried in the ledger today).
- Per-warehouse stock checks in Manufacturing (global quantity is checked).
