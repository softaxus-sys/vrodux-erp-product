# Real Estate

**Headline:** One listing, one unit — plus rent that chases itself.
**Tagline:** Stock listings, leases, rent schedules, tenant reminders, renewals and terminations.

## Features
- **Properties & Units (merged)** — one listing = a property + a unit; create the building as you add the unit (a same-named building is reused). Property types are free text. Covers rental and sale stock: purpose, listed date, bedrooms, price/rent, area, media, owner and agent details, raw sheet cells kept alongside parsed figures.
- **Multi-select filters** — building, bedrooms, type, status, furnishing, city, agent, price range; removable chips.
- **Import** rental/sale stock sheets from Excel/CSV (sale prices go to asking price, not rent; occupancy read from the price cell).
- **Leases** — create with tenant and vacant unit; payment frequency (monthly/quarterly/semi-annual/annual); a **rent schedule** is generated and sums exactly; record payments, waive, advance rent at signing (cascades across instalments).
- **Reminders (email)** — rent due soon (e.g. 30/7/1 days), overdue chasers (repeat and cap), lease-expiry notices (90/60/30); CC list; preview and "run now"; each notice is sent once; stops when paid. Time zone configurable. Off until configured; reports honestly if no mail account is set.
- **"About to fall vacant" alerts** — in-app notifications to the unit's agent and administrators (no email).
- **Renew** a lease (links to the old one, new schedule, unpaid rent carries over) and **terminate** (date, reason, final account = deposit + paid − owed − penalty − deductions; later date = notice; settle the refund).
- **Tenants, brokers, sales, property profile** (photos, website publishing, printable profile).
- Rent can be posted to Finance (Finance Posting).

## Access control
Permissions for properties, units, tenants, contracts, brokers, sales, rent (view/record/remind), alerts.
