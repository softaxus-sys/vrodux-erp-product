# Industry Packs: B2B, Education, Healthcare, Insurance

These four live alongside CRM and have their own permissions. Each has a **summary** tab and three working lists. Keep these pages short — they are lighter than the main modules.

## B2B
Proposals → Contracts → Support tickets. Create, update status, delete; summary overview.

## Education
Admissions → Students → Enrollments. Enroll, update status, summary.

## Healthcare
Patients → Appointments → Treatment plans. Complete appointments, update status, summary.

## Insurance
Policies → Renewals → Claims. Renew, record payments, resolve/approve claims (claims have an approve permission), summary.

## Access control
Each sub-feature has view/create/edit/delete permissions (claims also approve), customisable per role.

## Do not claim yet
- These are inline-table views, not full-depth products: no patient records/clinical notes, no student grading/timetables, no insurer integrations, no document generation.
- Tabs show even without view permission (the list then errors); status quick-actions aren't hidden by permission on the screen (enforced on the server).
- Not audited to the same depth as the main modules; also, Construction and Recipe exist as services but have no verified documentation here — **do not publish pages for them until reviewed.**
