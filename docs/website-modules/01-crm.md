# CRM

**Headline:** Capture every lead, work it as a team, close it, and see exactly what's working.
**Tagline:** Leads, opportunities, accounts and activities in one place — with team-based visibility and real reports.

## Overview
A full sales CRM: leads become accounts, contacts and opportunities; every touch is logged on one timeline; managers see who is closing what.

## Who it's for
Sales teams, agencies, real-estate brokers, service businesses — anyone with inbound enquiries and a team that works them.

## Features
**Leads**
- Lead list, kanban board (new / contacted / qualified / converted) with drag-and-drop, search, status filters, infinite scroll.
- Automatic 0–100 lead score based on contactability, purchase urgency, budget, intent keywords, source quality, priority and engagement; Hot / Warm / Cold banding; hottest-first sorting.
- Captured requirements: WhatsApp, budget, interested-in, message, planned purchase timeframe, plus marketing attribution (platform, campaign, ad, form) and form responses.
- Budget text such as "50k–100k", "1.5M", "5 lakh", "2 crore" is understood; value is derived only when it can be trusted.
- Import from Excel/CSV with automatic column matching and duplicate detection.
- Convert a lead into account + contact + opportunity in one step.

**Pipeline / opportunities**
- Board and list, stage moves with drag-and-drop, forecast categories (pipeline / best case / commit / closed), weighted value, loss-reason capture.
- Link opportunities to accounts; multiple contacts per deal with buying roles (decision maker, champion, etc.).

**Accounts and contacts**
- Account drawer with contacts, linked opportunities, an account-level timeline that rolls up its deals' and leads' activity.

**Activities**
- Calls, meetings, emails, notes and tasks; meeting/call time; assign tasks to a teammate (they get a notification).

**Quotations from CRM** (needs the Sales module)
- Create a quotation for a lead or account from a Sales template, preview the branded document, email it, copy the customer link or download a PDF.

**Documents** — attach files to leads, opportunities and accounts.

**Teams and visibility**
- Three access tiers per area: everyone, team (a team lead sees their team's records), assigned-only (a rep sees only their own).
- Assign or reassign leads with a team-grouped picker; every handover is recorded; supervisors are notified when work lands on their team member; bulk "file to team" on leads, opportunities and accounts.

**Integrations (lead sources)**
- Meta (Facebook/Instagram lead ads), website forms (a hosted form you can embed), custom API/webhooks, Zapier/Make, Google Forms/Sheets (via a small script), Calendly, Property Finder, Bayut/Dubizzle (pull), CSV/Excel import. Each has a built-in setup guide. Duplicate rules and assignment routing apply to every source.
- If a portal feed stops working, admins get an email and in-app alert (and one when it recovers).

**Reports (8)** — Sales pipeline, Win/Loss, Team performance (grouped by team), Lead source ROI, Lead conversion, Sales velocity, Activities, Account revenue. Filter by date, owner, stage; export CSV/PDF. Results respect each person's access tier.

## Works with
Sales (quotations), Finance (visa/other invoicing links), Visa Services (create a visa case from a lead), Lead Generation (push prospects into CRM), notifications (real-time bell and toast), File Manager, Reports hub, AI Assistant, mobile app.

## Access control
Separate permissions for leads, pipeline, customers, reports (view/export), integrations. Roles are customisable per customer.

## Do not claim yet
- Automatic assignment of integration-captured leads to a specific user by routing rules is name-based only (admins reassign).
- Workflow/automation engine, configurable pipelines, renewals/health scores, cases/tickets, AI scoring — planned, not built.
- Stage history for reports accrues from the date the feature was deployed; older closed deals use approximate dates.
