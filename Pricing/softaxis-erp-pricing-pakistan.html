📋 Important Terms & Conditions
  1. All prices are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR) and exclusive of applicable taxes (GST/SST). Federal or provincial tax will be charged separately as per prevailing rates.
  2. Annual plans are billed in full upfront at the annual rate. Monthly plans are billed on the 1st of each month.
  3. One-Time License grants perpetual use of the version purchased. The Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) covers software updates, bug fixes, and support. AMC renewal is optional but recommended.
  4. Annual subscriptions include a 17% discount versus equivalent monthly billing over 12 months.
  5. Pricing is subject to annual revision. Existing clients will receive 60 days' notice before any price change.
  6. Free trial available for 14 days — no credit card required. Trial includes Business plan features.
  7. Refund policy: Monthly subscriptions — cancel anytime, no refund for current period. Annual — pro-rata refund within first 30 days of billing.
  8. On-premise deployment is available for Business and Enterprise plans at an additional server licensing fee (starting PKR 30,000 one-time).
