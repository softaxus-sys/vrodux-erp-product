VAT Notice — Federal Tax Authority (FTA)
All prices listed in this document are exclusive of UAE VAT (5%). VAT will be applied to software subscription fees and professional services as per UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 and FTA guidelines. A valid VAT-compliant tax invoice will be issued for every transaction. Clients eligible for input VAT credit may recover the tax through their standard FTA VAT return.

📋 Important Terms & Conditions
  1. All prices are in UAE Dirhams (AED) and exclusive of UAE VAT at 5%. VAT is applied in accordance with Federal Decree-Law No. 8 of 2017 on Value Added Tax. A VAT-compliant tax invoice is issued for every charge.
  2. Annual plans are billed in full upfront at the annual rate, giving a 17% saving versus monthly billing. Monthly plans are billed on the 1st of each month.
  3. One-Time License grants a perpetual right to use the purchased version. The Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) covers updates, security patches, and support. AMC renewal is optional but strongly recommended.
  4. Pricing is subject to annual revision. Existing clients will receive 60 days' written notice before any price change takes effect.
  5. Free trial: 14 days at Business plan level — no credit card required. Trial data can be retained upon subscription.
  6. Refund policy: Monthly — cancel anytime; no refund for current billing period. Annual — pro-rata refund within 30 days of annual billing date only.
  7. On-premise deployment is available for Business and Enterprise plans. Server licensing fee starts at AED 2,500 one-time per server node.
  8. All contractual agreements, SLAs, and data processing agreements are governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates.
