## 5. Account deletion (Play requirement)

Play requires, for apps that let people create accounts:
1. A way to request deletion **inside the app**, and
2. A **web URL** that explains how to request deletion (entered in the Data safety form).

Status today (**YOU / me to build**):
- https://vrodux.com/delete-account currently returns **404** — it must exist before you submit.
- **In-app (done):** Settings → Delete account → *Request account deletion* sends a high-priority support ticket (category Account & Security) with the user's name, email and workspace, after a confirmation. Nothing is deleted by the tap itself; the team verifies and completes it. In Vrodux a person's login belongs to their company's workspace, so the workspace administrator removes users and the company owner can delete the workspace.

Recommended wording for the page (publish on vrodux.com):
```
Delete your Vrodux ERP account or data

Vrodux accounts belong to a company workspace.
• To remove your own login: ask your workspace administrator (Settings → Users), or email vrodux@softaxis.ae from the address on the account.
• To delete the whole workspace and all its data: the workspace owner emails vrodux@softaxis.ae from the owner address, stating the workspace name. We verify ownership and complete deletion within 30 days.
What is deleted: the user account, sign-in sessions, device and push tokens, and (for a workspace deletion) all business records.
What may be kept: records we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons (for example billing records), for the legally required period.
```
**YOU:** confirm the retention period and the real support address before publishing; the 30-day figure above is a placeholder, not a policy I verified. I can add an **in-app "Request account deletion"** button in Settings (opens that page / pre-filled email) — say if you want it; it is the safest way to satisfy the in-app requirement.
